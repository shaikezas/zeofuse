Centralized Catalog Management

Build your product catalog once in Zeofuse. Every channel receives your content in the exact format it requires — automatically.

  • Import from ERP, PIM, Shopify, WooCommerce, or Excel
  • Map your attributes to each channel's schema once
  • Bulk edit products across all channels simultaneously
  • Content syndication keeps every channel up to date
Rules-Based Pricing Engine

Set your pricing rules once. Zeofuse automatically adjusts prices per channel to keep you competitive while protecting your margins.

  • Define min/max price boundaries per product
  • Automatic repricing when competitors change their prices
  • Set different price strategies per channel or region
  • Promotional pricing with scheduled start/end dates
Unified Order Management (OMS)

All your orders from every channel flow into one inbox. No more logging into 10 different seller portals to process your day's orders.

  • Single order dashboard across all channels
  • Automatic order routing to the right warehouse or fulfillment partner
  • Real-time inventory deduction across all channels on sale
  • Shipping updates pushed back to each channel automatically
Cross-Channel Analytics

See exactly what's selling, where, and at what margin — across every channel in a single view. Make decisions with real data.

  • Revenue and margin breakdown per channel
  • Best-performing products by marketplace
  • Inventory turnover and stock-out alerts
  • Custom reports exportable in multiple formats
