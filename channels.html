The Problem

Onboarding suppliers at scale is broken

Every marketplace faces the same challenge — sourcing quality suppliers is slow, messy, and expensive without the right infrastructure.

Supplier onboarding takes weeks of back-and-forth

Getting a new supplier's product data into your system typically involves email threads, spreadsheet templates, and a lot of manual data cleaning.

Product data quality is inconsistent

Every supplier formats their data differently. Your team spends hours normalizing, reformatting, and fixing product information before anything can go live.

Order routing to suppliers is manual

Sending orders to suppliers via email, WhatsApp, or spreadsheet is error-prone, slow, and impossible to scale beyond a handful of suppliers.

Real-time stock visibility is near-impossible

You're constantly showing products as "in stock" that your suppliers have already sold elsewhere. Cancellations damage your marketplace's reputation.

Scaling supplier count multiplies operational complexity

Going from 10 suppliers to 100 suppliers doesn't just mean 10x the product count — it means 10x the operational complexity with no scalable system in place.

No standardized supplier performance tracking

Which suppliers ship on time? Which ones have quality issues? Without a unified system, this data is buried in separate reports and support tickets.

How Zeofuse Works for You

Your marketplace. Zeofuse's supplier network.

Zeofuse acts as the middleware between your marketplace and a growing base of connected suppliers. One API integration gives you access to everything.

01
Integrate once via REST API

Connect your marketplace platform to Zeofuse with a single API integration. We provide full API documentation and an integration team to help you go live fast.

02
Access the Zeofuse supplier catalog

Once connected, browse and approve suppliers from the Zeofuse network. Their products arrive in your system in a standardized format — ready to publish immediately.

03
Orders route automatically

When a customer places an order on your marketplace, Zeofuse routes it to the right supplier automatically. Confirmations, tracking updates, and fulfillment status flow back to you in real time.

04
Monitor supplier performance

Track fulfillment rates, response times, return rates, and product quality scores across all your suppliers from a single dashboard. Set SLAs and get alerts automatically.
