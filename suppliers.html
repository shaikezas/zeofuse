The Problem

Selling on multiple channels shouldn't be this hard

Most suppliers today manage their multi-channel presence with a mess of spreadsheets, manual uploads, and separate logins — for every single platform.

Every platform has a different format

Amazon wants one spreadsheet structure. Noon wants another. Zalando has a third. Keeping them all in sync means constant manual reformatting.

Orders are scattered across 10 portals

Your team logs into each marketplace separately every morning. A missed order on one platform means an angry customer and a bad rating.

Stock-outs happen because inventory isn't synced

You sell your last 2 units on Amazon, but Noon still shows them in stock. You get a cancellation, a penalty, and a damaged seller rating.

Pricing is manual and always out of date

Competitors change prices daily. Updating your prices across 5 channels manually is a half-day job — and by the time you're done, prices have changed again.

Growing to a new channel takes weeks

Every new marketplace is a new setup, new catalog mapping, new integration, new portal login. Expanding your reach is a project, not a decision.

You can't see what's actually working

Your data lives in 6 different places. Getting a clear view of which products are profitable on which channels requires hours of spreadsheet work.

The Zeofuse Solution

One platform.
Every problem solved.

Zeofuse is the single backend for your entire multi-channel selling operation. Connect your catalog once and let Zeofuse handle everything that happens downstream.

01
Import your catalog

Connect your existing ERP, PIM, Shopify store, WooCommerce, or simply upload a spreadsheet. Zeofuse imports your products, images, prices, and stock in one shot.

02
Map your catalog attributes once

Tell Zeofuse which of your attributes maps to each channel's required fields. You do this once per channel — Zeofuse remembers and applies it forever.

03
Set your pricing rules

Define your minimum margins and pricing strategies. Zeofuse automatically adjusts your prices to stay competitive across every channel within your boundaries.

04
Go live — orders and inventory run automatically

Your listings go live across all channels. Every order lands in your Zeofuse OMS inbox. Inventory updates automatically every time you sell anywhere. You just ship.

Set rules. Zeofuse enforces them. Automatic repricing keeps you competitive across every channel without any manual intervention.
